Huddle

Giving Lifetime's internal communications an identity that a busy, widely spread team actually notices.

+40%satisfaction with internal comms

Lifetime Training
Year

My role
Named and designed the brand
Scope

Brief

One voice for
a widely spread team.

Lifetime's internal communications had no consistent identity, and messages felt top down and easy to ignore across a busy and widely spread team.

What I did

Built with colleagues,
made for everyone.

I spoke to colleagues across departments, named the brand and developed the logo, visual language and guidelines, along with ready to use templates for newsletters, presentations and posters so anyone could stay on brand without a designer.

Listening firstConversations with colleagues across departments shaped the brand.
Name and identityThe Huddle name, logo, visual language and guidelines.
TemplatesNewsletters, presentations and posters anyone can use.

The mark

Four people,
one sphere.

The logo's interlocking shapes stand for teamwork and connection, reflecting the portal's purpose of bringing every employee together in one place.

The portal

Where the brand
lives every day.

Huddle became the identity of Lifetime's intranet, giving news, policies, benefits and events one recognisable home.

The system

Playful,
but professional.

A palette of blue, pink and green with geometric patterns keeps the brand welcoming and energetic, while the clear type and layouts keep it professional.

Result

Communications people engage with.

25%More participation in internal channels
40%Higher satisfaction with internal comms
3Template sets: newsletters, presentations and posters

Why it mattered: colleagues engaged more with what they received, and anyone in the business could now create on brand communications without waiting for a designer.
