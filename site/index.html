Approach

Strategy first,
then craft that
holds up.

Most of my work sits between strategy and making. The same four steps run through a rebrand, a national campaign or a single animation.

  1. Understand the problem

    Every brief starts with what has to change for the business, whether that is how a brand is perceived, how many people apply, or a partnership that is at risk.

    Seen in the Lifetime brand audit

  2. Set a clear direction

    I turn research into a small number of distinct routes and present them to boards and senior stakeholders with real executions, so decisions are made on how the work will look in use.

    Seen in three directions for the Lifetime board

  3. Build it as a system

    Guidelines, templates, asset libraries and AI enabled tools mean the work keeps its quality when a whole team uses it, long after launch.

    Seen in Huddle and the Learning Awards templates

  4. Measure what changed

    I track the result that mattered to the brief, from applications and vacancies to participation and board decisions, and use it to shape the next piece of work.

    Seen in #IAspireToBe and B&Q Reignite
